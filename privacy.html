Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Quillfeather Studio (“we”, “us” or “our”) is a small business operated by the owner of the Etsy shop QuillfeatherStudios and the Pinterest account quillfeatherstudio. This policy explains how we handle information through this website, our shop and customer-support activities, and the private tools we use with Etsy, Pinterest, email and related service providers.

This website

This website is static. It has no customer accounts, forms, comments, advertising or payment processing, and it does not intentionally set cookies. Our hosting provider, Vercel, may automatically process standard technical details, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and requested page, to deliver the site, prevent abuse and maintain security. We do not use those logs to build visitor profiles.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request, provide support, protect our business and comply with applicable obligations.

Our Etsy and Pinterest tools

We use private tools with our own Etsy and Pinterest business accounts. The tools are used only for Quillfeather Studio and are not offered to the public. Platform authorization is handled through each platform’s approved authorization process; we do not collect platform passwords or browser session cookies.

  • Etsy: our tools may access information Etsy permits about our own listings, orders and sales, create or update listings for our shop, and help us measure product performance. Customer-support communications may be received through Etsy’s services or through the business email address associated with our shop.
  • Pinterest: our tools may create and schedule Pins and boards on our own account and access analytics about our own Pins and boards. The owner reviews and approves each Pin before it is published or scheduled. Our tools do not automatically follow accounts, send messages, post comments or manufacture engagement.

We access platform data only as needed to operate and measure our own accounts. We do not sell platform API data, share it for third-party advertising, use it to profile unrelated users, or use it to train public or third-party artificial-intelligence models. We retain raw platform data only when permitted and needed; wherever practical, we keep limited derived or aggregate performance records instead.

Customer and order information

When you buy from our Etsy shop or request support, Etsy or you may provide information such as your name, email address, order details, product purchased and message. We use this information to fulfill and support purchases, diagnose product problems, detect recurring support issues, prevent fraud, keep business records and meet legal or tax obligations. Etsy’s own privacy policy governs information Etsy collects directly from you.

Email, automation and AI-assisted support

We use business email and private automation tools to organize messages, prepare responses, monitor product performance and identify recurring issues. Some tasks may use AI-assisted services acting for Quillfeather Studio. We provide only the information reasonably needed for the task, review or restrict sensitive and high-impact actions, and require refunds, disputes, legal complaints and similar escalations to receive owner attention.

We do not intentionally use customer communications or order information to train public or third-party AI models. Service providers may process information on our behalf under their own contractual and security terms. They may include Etsy, Pinterest, Google for business email, Vercel for website hosting, and providers used for private business automation and AI-assisted processing.

Why we process information

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we process information to perform a purchase or provide requested support, pursue legitimate business interests such as operating and improving our products and preventing abuse, comply with legal obligations, or act with consent where consent is required.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the business, with professional advisers when necessary, when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security. We do not share customer information for unrelated third-party advertising.

Retention and security

We keep order, accounting and tax records for the periods required by law. We keep support communications and operational logs only as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, identify recurring problems, maintain records and protect the business. Retention periods may vary because Etsy and other service providers maintain their own records under their policies.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including restricted account access and approved platform authorization methods. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to provide, correct or delete personal information we control, or to restrict or object to certain processing where applicable. You may also request a portable copy where applicable and withdraw consent for future processing when consent is the legal basis. Some information may need to be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

To make a request, email quillfeatherstudio@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also contact the privacy or data-protection authority that applies where you live.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, those providers are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Children

This website, our products and our tools are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we will post the revised version here and update the date above.
